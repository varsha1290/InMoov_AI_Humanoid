# InMoov assembly photographs

The repository's photo documentation is intended to use the two original photographs supplied for this project, not generated or stock images.

- Assembly overview: shows the head, neck, orange torso frame, white lower torso structure and a visible arm/shoulder assembly.
- Torso front detail: shows the orange upper torso frame, visible markings including T3R, T3L and T5, and the white lower torso structure.

These photographs are visual documentation only. They do not establish exact servo counts, wiring, joint axes, dimensions or calibration values.
